01 · The Concept

A dressing room that never says “you’re ready.”

Vanity · mirror · makeup · perfume · jewelry · hair accessories · pink light · soft music

“You look beautiful.”

Maybe a little more blush. · What about your hair? · Try another shade. · Almost ready. · One more thing.

BE NATURAL.MAKE MORE EFFORT.
STAND OUT.DON’T TRY TOO HARD.
BE CONFIDENT.FIX THIS FIRST.
Enter→Get ready→Interact→More advice→Contradiction→Realization→Leave
The only way to finish getting ready is to stop listening.

Lauren Lee McCarthy

LAUREN, 2017

Add documentation · LAUREN Testimonials film & 360 photography by David Leonard

McCarthy installs cameras, microphones and smart devices in people’s homes and attempts to become a human version of Amazon Alexa — exploring intimacy vs privacy, convenience vs agency.

MCCARTHYA system tries to help and respond to you.
MY PROJECTA system gives you a task that can never be completed.

Sources: artist’s statements via Gazelli Art House, Barbican, MIT Docubase

07 · Who am I assuming my audience is?

Assumption → refinement

ASSUMEDEveryone understands “getting ready”No beauty knowledge required
ASSUMEDEveryone wears makeupNothing needs to be applied
ASSUMEDVisitors obey the mirrorRefusing is a valid interaction
ASSUMEDVisitors sit at the vanityResponds to standing or observing
ASSUMEDEveryone feels beauty pressure similarlyNever classifies identity or appearance
ASSUMEDVisitors stay long enoughThe contradiction appears quickly
ASSUMEDResistance = the artwork failedResistance may be the best interaction
If someone ignores the mirror and walks out, they have already answered the question.
The installation should not depend on my audience behaving exactly how I expect.
